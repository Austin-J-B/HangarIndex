# Public maintenance and supplier starter corpus

This proof-of-concept set contains 39 public reference items gathered on 2026-10-05 through 2026-10-07. It spans FAA, EASA, Spain's AESA, China's CAAC, Transport Canada, aluminum and titanium materials, corrosion and coating references, and Lufthansa Technik supplier context. It is a discovery demo, not a mirror of company-controlled maintenance data.

## Applying this corpus

- Regulatory requirements depend on the authority, certificate, scope, part, aircraft, and work order. Do not assume that rules or approvals transfer between authorities.
- Advisory circulars, AMC/GM, standards, supplier manuals, and product listings have different legal force. Check the controlling source and approved data for the actual task.
- The corpus contains no company MOE/RSM/QCM, CMMs, AMMs, SRMs, repair routings, task cards, approved parts lists, or Lufthansa-controlled technical manuals.
- Files and source pages can change. Use the linked authority or manufacturer page to check currency, revision, and applicability.
- NASA papers include historical and experimental work; results do not establish a production process or an approved aerospace maintenance method.
